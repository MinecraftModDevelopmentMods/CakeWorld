CakeWorld 0.2.0.118021

- Expands CakeWorld into an edible Overworld, Nether, and End with themed
  biomes, terrain, fluids, structures, creatures, foods, and gadgets.
- Adds the Explorer's Cookbook discovery system and child-friendly mob
  behaviour on Easy and Normal difficulty.
- Adds themed vanilla ores and optional Base Metals compatibility.
- Uses the published MMD OreSpawn 4.0.16.118021 integration.
- Adopts the MMD Java namespace, target-qualified version format,
  ForgeGradle 7 build, and guarded release pipeline.

CakeWorld remains an alpha. Original artwork, audio, balance, accessibility,
multiplayer, and family playtesting are still in progress.
